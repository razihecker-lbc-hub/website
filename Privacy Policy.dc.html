Privacy Policy

Families trust us with grades, goals, essays and stories that are personal. This page explains, in plain language, what we collect, why we collect it, and how we keep it private.

Effective November 3, 2026

The short version

Your story stays yours.

We never sell your information. We never share a student's name, essays, photos, results or testimonial publicly without explicit permission. Every student essay, photo, quote and acceptance you see on our website or social media is shared with the permission of the student and their family.

01Who we are

League Bound Consulting ("League Bound," "we," "us") is a boutique college admissions consulting firm. We work one-on-one with students and their families on college guidance, applications and essays. This policy covers our website, leagueboundconsulting.com, the forms you fill out on our site or through our ads on platforms like LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram, our free consultations, and the services we provide to our clients.

02What we collect

What you tell us. When you request a consultation, book a call, download a resource or fill out one of our forms, we collect what you share with us: usually a parent's and student's name, email address, phone number, the student's school and graduation year, and what you're hoping to get help with.

What we work on together. If you become a client, we'll also see the materials that go into an application: grades, test scores, activities, awards, school lists, essay drafts and the personal stories behind them. We use these only to help the student.

What our website collects automatically. Like most websites, ours uses cookies and similar tools, including the Meta Pixel, to understand how visitors use the site and to measure our advertising. These can include analytics and advertising tools from companies such as Google, Meta and LinkedIn. They collect things like your browser type, the pages you visit and how you arrived on our site.

03How we use it

We use your information to respond to you, schedule and run your consultation, deliver the services you sign up for, and send you occasional updates and resources about college admissions. You can unsubscribe from our emails at any time using the link at the bottom of any email, or by asking us directly. We also use website and ad data to understand which of our content is helpful, so we can improve our site and show our ads to the right families.

04Nothing shared without your permission

A student's work is private. We do not publish or share a student's name, photo, essays, school list, results or testimonial outside of our team unless the student and family have given us explicit permission to do so. For students under 18, we ask for a parent's or guardian's permission as well.

When you do give permission, we use only what you've agreed to, in the way you've agreed to. You can change your mind at any time. Just let us know and we'll stop using it going forward and remove it from our website and social media.

05Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. Ever. We share it only in these limited cases:

Our team. The consultants and advisors working with your student see what they need to do that work, and they're expected to keep it confidential.

The tools we run our business on. Like any business, we use trusted service providers for things like scheduling (Calendly), email, video calls, document sharing, customer records and payments. They handle your information only to provide those services to us.

When the law requires it. We may disclose information if we're legally required to, or to protect the safety and rights of our students, clients or team.

06Students under 18

Most of the students we work with are in high school, so a parent or guardian is part of the process from the start. Our website and ads are aimed at parents and students of high school age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

07How we protect and keep it

We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure, including limiting access to the people who need it and using reputable, secure tools. No system is perfectly secure, but we treat your family's information the way we'd want ours treated. We keep information for as long as we need it to work with you and for our legitimate business and legal records, and then we delete it.

08Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see the information we have about you or your student, correct it, or delete it. You can unsubscribe from our emails whenever you like, and you can limit cookies through your browser settings or the ad settings on platforms like Google, Meta and LinkedIn. Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights under your state's law, including the right to know what we collect and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We don't sell it, and we'll honor any request. To make one, just email us.

09Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we'll post the new version here and change the effective date at the top. If we make a significant change to how we use your information, we'll let our clients know directly.
